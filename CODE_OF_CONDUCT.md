# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository is part of a professional agency working on behalf of client
businesses. Everyone contributing here is expected to act with the same care and
discretion they would bring to a client's office.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors, clients, client customers, and reviewers with respect.
- Give and take technical feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep client information, lead data, and credentials confidential.
- Assume good faith and ask a direct question instead of speculating in public.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing client data, credentials, secrets, or internal operational details
  outside the group that needs them.
- Deliberately bypassing review, validation, or the safety requirements of this
  repository.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity: issues, pull requests, review
comments, commit messages, and other project communication. It also applies when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to team@movermarketing.ai or to a maintainer directly. Do not open
a public issue about another person's conduct.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review the report, speak
with the people involved, and decide on a response, which may range from a
private conversation to removing access.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close contributions, or revoke access
for behavior that breaks this policy. A maintainer who is the subject of a report
will not take part in deciding the outcome.
